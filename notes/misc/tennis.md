# Serve

## technique 

- loading: 
	- weight is loaded toward the back/right leg
	- shoulders-hips coil
- 
	- start tossing
	- weight transfer to front foot (left) 
- throphy possion: 
	- racket is up/back
	- knees are bent
	- left arm is fully extended
	- ball has just released
	- shoulders are coiled
- drive upward:
	- push from the legs
	- hips/torso uncoil
	- racket is droped behind the back
	- left arm starts coming down
	- hitting elbow rises
	- body drives upward and forward
- contact:
	- arm is nearly extended
	- racket is moving very fast
	- ball is slightly in front of you
- follow-through:
	- racket continues across/down
	- torso continues rotating
	- body continues forward
- landing:
	- land on the left/front foot

## tips

- **serve to the body**: aiming at your opponent’s belly button makes it difficult for them to return effectively, often leading to a weak response you can attack. https://youtu.be/ByNtBs8-lJM?t=6

## Target

**hit to the opponent standing where you are standing.**

### as a baseliner

hit cross-court to the opponent's baseliner to prolong the point.
**exception**: If the opposing net player continuously poaches/intercepts your crosscourt groundstrokes, occasionally hit a shot down the line (aiming inside the singles line for margin
### as a net player

aim for the opposing net player (preferably low at their feet) to finish the point.

**exception**: If you have a low volley, avoid the net player and hit back to the baseliner to avoid getting slammed

## Net movement

- move toward the ball as it moves across the court
- when the ball moves away, the net player on that side shifts forward (offense), while the opposite net player drops back slightly (defense)

## Positioning after hitting a successful lob

When you lob over your opponents and they have to switch: 

you and your partner should move to the service line

## Defense when getting lobbed

- when you lobed over your head: move back diagonally into "no-man’s land" to gain information.
	- if your P hit a weak lob: move all the way back to the baseline; 
	- if your P a good ball: you can move forward to maintain an offensive position.
