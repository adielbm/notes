
## B-field
$$\vec{\boldsymbol{B}}$$
- names: **magnetic field** (ambiguous), **magnetic flux density**, **magnetic induction**, **B-field**
- The SI unit: the **tesla** ($\mathrm{T}$) which is defined as $1 \, \text{T} = 1 \, \text{N}/(\text{A} \cdot \text{m})$

## H-field
$$\vec{\boldsymbol{H}}=\frac{1}{\mu_0}\vec{\boldsymbol{B}} - \vec{\boldsymbol{M}}$$
- names: **magnetic field intensity**, **magnetic field strength**, **magnetic field** (ambiguous)
- The SI unit: $\mathrm{A/m}$ 
- $\mu_0$ is the permeability of free space 
- $\vec{\boldsymbol{M}}$ is the magnetization of the material
- **permeability**


# Magnetic flux

- ${\displaystyle \Phi _{B}=\iint _{S}\vec{\boldsymbol{B}} \cdot d\vec{\boldsymbol{S}}}$ is the **magnetic flux** (in $\mathrm{Wb}$) through a surface $S$ in a magnetic field $\vec{\boldsymbol{B}}$.
	- $\displaystyle \Phi_B = \vec{\boldsymbol{B}} \cdot \vec{\boldsymbol{A}}$ is the magnetic flux through a flat surface with a vector area $\vec{\boldsymbol{A}}$ in a uniform magnetic field $\vec{\boldsymbol{B}}$.
	- see also: [[Maxwell's equations|Gauss's Law for magnetism]]


# Magnetic moment


- **magnetic dipole**
- $\vec{\boldsymbol{\mu}} = I \vec{\boldsymbol{A}}$ is the **magnetic dipole moment** (or **magnetic moment**) (in $\mathrm{A \cdot m^2}$) of a current loop with area $\vec{\boldsymbol{A}}$ carrying current $I$. 
- $\vec{\boldsymbol{\tau}}=\vec{\boldsymbol{\mu}} \times \vec{\boldsymbol{B}}$ is the torque on a magnetic dipole $\vec{\boldsymbol{\mu}}$ in a magnetic field $\vec{\boldsymbol{B}}$.
- $U=-\vec{\boldsymbol{\mu}} \cdot \vec{\boldsymbol{B}}$ is the potential energy of a magnetic dipole $\vec{\boldsymbol{\mu}}$ in a magnetic field $\vec{\boldsymbol{B}}$.
