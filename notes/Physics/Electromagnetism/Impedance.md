
- $Z = R + jX$ is the **impedance** (in $\Omega$)  
	- $R$ is the **resistance** (in $\Omega$)  
	- $X$ is the **reactance** (in $\Omega$), where:  
		- $X_L = \omega L$ is the **inductive reactance**  
		- $X_C = \frac{1}{\omega C}$ is the **capacitive reactance**  
		- $X = X_L - X_C$  
	- $|Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + X^2}$ is the magnitude of impedance  
	- $\tan \phi = \frac{X}{R}$ is the phase angle  
	- SI unit: **ohm** ($\Omega$) 
		- $1\,\Omega=1\,\mathrm{kg   \cdot m^2\cdot s^{−3}\cdot A^{-2}}$
- $Y = G + jB$ is the **admittance** (in S, siemens)  
	- $G = \frac{1}{R}$ is the **conductance**  
	- $B$ is the **susceptance**, where:  
		- $B_L = -\frac{1}{X_L} = -\frac{1}{\omega L}$ is the **inductive susceptance**  
		- $B_C = \frac{1}{X_C} = \omega C$ is the **capacitive susceptance**  
		- $B = B_C + B_L$  
	- $|Y| = \sqrt{G^2 + B^2}$ is the magnitude of admittance  
	- $\tan \phi = \frac{B}{G}$ is the phase angle  


- $Z_\text{series} = Z_1 + Z_2 + \dots + Z_n$
- $Z_\text{parallel} = \left( \frac{1}{Z_1} + \frac{1}{Z_2} + \dots + \frac{1}{Z_n} \right)^{-1}$
