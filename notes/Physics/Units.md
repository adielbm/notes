## SI base units

|                | **SI unit**  | dimension         | quantities                       |
| -------------- | ------------ | ----------------- | -------------------------------- |
| $\mathrm{s}$   | **second**   | $\mathrm{T}$      | time $t$                         |
| $\mathrm{m}$   | **metre**    | $\mathrm{L}$      | length $l, r, x$                 |
| $\mathrm{kg}$  | **kilogram** | $\mathrm{M}$      | mass $m$                         |
| $\mathrm{A}$   | **ampere**   | $\mathrm{I}$      | electric current $I, i$          |
| $\mathrm{K}$   | **kelvin**   | $\mathrm{\Theta}$ | thermodynamic temperature $T$    |
| $\mathrm{mol}$ | **mole**     | $\mathrm{N}$      | amount of substance $n$          |
| $\mathrm{cd}$  | **candela**  | $\mathrm{J}$      | luminous intensity $I_{\rm {v}}$ |

## Energy 


- **joule** $\mathrm{J}$ 
	- $\mathrm{kg   \cdot m^2\cdot s^{−2}}$
- (**kilowatt-hour**)
	- $\mathrm{1\ kWh = 3.6\times 10^6\ J}$
	- the energy consumed by a 1-kilowatt appliance in 1 hour
- (**electronvolt**)
	- $\mathrm{1\ eV = 1.6\times 10^{-19}\ J}$
	- the energy acquired by a particle carrying a charge whose magnitude equals that on the electron as a result of moving through a potential difference of $1\ \mathsf{V}$
#### calorie

- in physics/chemistry: **small calorie**, **gram calorie**, **thermochemical calorie**
	- $1\,\mathrm{cal}=4.184\,\mathrm{J}$
- in nutrition: **large calorie**, **kilocalorie**, **Calorie**, **kilogram calorie**
	- $1\,\mathrm{Cal}=4.184\,\mathrm{kJ}$ (also $\mathrm{kcal}$)

## decibels

- A **power quantity** is a physical quantity $X$ that is directly proportional to power: $X\propto P$
	- examples: power, energy density, acoustic intensity
- A **root-power quantity** (or [*field quantity*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power,_root-power,_and_field_quantities)) is a physical quantity $X$ whose square is directly proportional to power: $X^2\propto P$ (or $X\propto \sqrt{ P }$) 
	- examples: voltage, current, sound pressure, electric field strength, speed, or charge density
- **bel** ($\mathrm{B}$)
- **decibel** (**dB**): $1\,\mathrm{dB}=0.1\,\mathrm{B}$
- for any power quantities $P$ and $P_0$ (reference):
	- $\displaystyle L_{P} = 10 \log_{10}\left(\frac{P}{P_0}\right)\,\mathrm{dB}=\log_{10}\left(\frac{P}{P_0}\right)\,\mathrm{B}$
	- $P$ and $P_0$ said to differ by $L_P$ decibels
	- $L_P$ is a **power level**
	- examples:
		- [[Sound|sound power level]]
		- [[sound|sound intensity level]]
		- **decibel watt** (dBW): ${\text{power}\,[\mathrm{dBW}]}=10\log _{10}{\frac {\text{power}\,[\mathrm{W}]}{1\,\mathrm {W} }}$
		- **dBm** (or **dBmW**): ${\text{power}\,[\mathrm{dBm}]}=10\log _{10}{\frac {\text{power}\,[\mathrm{mW}]}{1\,\mathrm {mW} }}$
- for any root-power quantities $F$ and $F_0$ (reference):
	- $\displaystyle L_{F} = 20 \log_{10}\left(\frac{F}{F_0}\right)\,\mathrm{dB}=10 \log_{10}\left(\frac{F^2}{F_0^2}\right)\,\mathrm{dB}=2\log_{10}\left(\frac{F}{F_0}\right)\,\mathrm{B}$
	- $L_F$ is a **root-power level** (or **field level**)
	- examples:
		- [[sound|sound pressure level]]
		- **decibel volts**: $\text{voltage}\,[\mathrm{dBV}] = 20\log_{10}{\frac{\text{voltage}\,[\mathrm{V}]}{1\,\mathrm{V}}}$
		- **dBu**: $\text{voltage}_{\text{rms}}\,[\mathrm{dBu}] = 20\log_{10}{\frac{\text{voltage}_{\text{rms}}\,[\mathrm{V}]}{\sqrt{ 0.0006 }\,\mathrm{V}}}$
