# Other units


## Energy 

- (**kilowatt-hour**)
	- $\mathrm{1\ kWh = 3.6\times 10^6\ J}$
	- the energy consumed by a 1-kilowatt appliance in 1 hour
- (**electronvolt**)
	- $\mathrm{1\ eV = 1.6\times 10^{-19}\ J}$
	- the energy acquired by a particle carrying a charge whose magnitude equals that on the electron as a result of moving through a potential difference of $1\ \mathsf{V}$

## power

- ((imperial) **horsepower**, HP, BHP (brake horsepower)) $\mathrm{1\,hp}\approx 745.7\,\mathsf{W}$
- (**metric horsepower**, CV, PS) $\mathrm{1\,hp}\approx 735.5\,\mathsf{W}\approx 75\,\mathsf{kg_f\cdot m\cdot s^{-1}}$

## force

- (kilogram-force) $\mathrm{1\,kg_f}\approx 9.8\,\mathsf{N}$ 
- (pound-force) $\mathrm{1\,lb_f}\approx 4.4482\,\mathsf{N}$

## torque

- (kilogram-force meter, (קג"מ)) $\mathrm{1\,kg_f\cdot m}\approx 9.8\,\mathsf{N\cdot m}$
- (pound-force foot) $\mathrm{1\,lb_f\cdot ft}\approx 1.3558\,\mathsf{N\cdot m}$

## rotational frequency

- (revolutions per minute, $\mathrm{r \cdot min^{-1}}$) $\mathrm{1\, rpm} = \frac{1}{60}\,\mathsf{Hz}$


## decibels

- $L = 10 \log_{10}\left(\frac{P}{P_0}\right)$ 
	- $P$: power level
	- $P_0$: reference power level
- Sound Intensity Level


$L_I = 10 \, \log_{10}\!\left(\frac{I}{I_0}\right) \quad \text{dB}$



## calorie

- in physics/chemistry: **small calorie**, **gram calorie**, **thermochemical calorie**
	- $1\,\mathrm{cal}=4.184\,\mathrm{J}$
- in nutrition: **large calorie**, **kilocalorie**, **Calorie**, **kilogram calorie**
	- $1\,\mathrm{Cal}=4.184\,\mathrm{kJ}$
