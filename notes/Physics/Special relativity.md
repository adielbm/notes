- **relativistic energy** (אנרגייה יחסותית)
- **rest energy** (or **rest mass energy**)

- $E^{2}=(pc)^{2}+\left(mc^{2}\right)^{2}$
- $E = \gamma m c^2$ is the total relativistic energy
- $m$ is the rest mass

- $\displaystyle \gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - \frac{v^2}{c^2}}}$ is the **Lorentz factor**

- $p$ is the momentum
- $p = \gamma mv$
- $E_{0} = mc^2$ is the rest energy
- $K=E-E_0$ is the relativistic kinetic energy

- **time dilation** $\Delta t=\gamma \Delta t_{0}$
	- $\Delta t_0$ is the **proper time interval**
	- $\Delta t$ is the dilated time interval
- **length contraction** $L= \frac{L_{0}}{\gamma }$
	- $L_0$ is the **proper length** (or **rest length**)
	- $L$ is the contracted length

- atmospheric muons
	- $\Delta t_0$ is the lifetime of the muon in its own rest frame 
	- $\Delta t$ is the lifetime of the muon measured by the Earth observer
	- $L_0$ is the height of the atmosphere measured by the Earth observer
	- $L$ is the height of the atmosphere measured by the moving muon


- $\displaystyle u = \frac{v + u'}{1 + \frac{vu'}{c^2}}$
