
- $\displaystyle\rho = \frac{m}{V}$ is the **density** (or **volumetric mass density** or **specific mass**)
	- hebrew: צפיפות, צפיפות החומר, משקל סגולי, צפיפות המסה
	- SI unit: $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$, other units: $\mathrm{g/mL}$ and $\mathrm{g/L}$
- **specific weight** (or **unit weight**)
	- $\displaystyle \gamma =\frac {W}{V}=\rho g$
	- $W$ = weight, $V$ volume, $\rho$ density, $g$ [[Force#Gravity|standard gravity]]
- **relative density** (or **specific gravity**) (צפיפות יחסית) $\displaystyle\frac{\rho_{\text{substance}}}{\rho_{\text{reference}}}$ (dimensionless)
	- for liquids/solids: $\rho_{\text{reference}}$ is typically pure water at $4^\circ\text{C}$
	- for gases: $\rho_{\text{reference}}$ is typically air or hydrogen at a specified temperature
