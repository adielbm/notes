#### naming

- ions:
	- cations:
		- fixed charge metals: element name + _ion_ (e.g. $\ce{Na+}$, sodium ion)
		- variable charge metals: Roman numerals (e.g. $\ce{Fe^{3+}}$, iron(III) ion)
			- (common system: _-ous_ for lower and _-ic_ for higher oxidation state, e.g. Ferrous and Ferric for Iron(II) and Iron(III))
	- anions:
		- _ide_ suffix, (e.g. $\ce{Cl-}$ chloride, $\ce{O^{2-}}$ oxide)
- ionic compound
	- cation (metal) first
		- if it can have more than one ion use Roman numeral  
	- anion (non-metal) second
		- monatomic ions: first syllable + _ide_ 
		- polyatomic ions: name of the polyatomic ion
- molecular compound
	- first: prefix (unless it is one)
	- second: prefix and _ide_

	- "the small, dense region consisting of protons and neutrons at the center of the atom" (Wikipedia)
