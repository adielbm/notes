# naming

## ions

### cations

- fixed charge metals: element name + _ion_ (e.g. $\ce{Na+}$, sodium ion)
- variable charge metals: 
	- "modern naming": Roman numerals (e.g. $\ce{Fe^{3+}}$, iron(III) ion)
	- "old naming": 
		- _-ous_ for lower and _-ic_ for higher oxidation state, e.g. _ferrous_ and _ferric_ for iron(II) and iron(III))

### anions

- monatomic ions: **_ide_** suffix + "ion"
	- (e.g. $\ce{Cl-}$: _chloride ion_, $\ce{O^{2-}}$: _oxide ion_)
- polyatomic ions: 
	- see https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyatomic_ion#Nomenclature_of_polyatomic_anions)

## ionic compound
$$\displaystyle\text{cation anion}$$
- cation (metal) first (as in [[#cations]], but without "ion" ending)
- anion (non-metal) second

## molecular compound

- first: prefix-element (if > 1)
- second: prefix and **_ide_** suffix
