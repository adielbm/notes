# Nucleotides

- **nucleotides**
	- consists of:
		- a nucleotide base
		- [[#Carbohydrate|pentose]] (suger) 
			- e.g. deoxyribose (in DNA), ribose (in RNA) 
		- a phosphate group consisting of one to three phosphates
	- examples: 
		- [[Biochemistry#Coenzymes|ATP]]
		- DNA and RNA nucleotides


- a **nucleotide base** (or **nucleobase**, **nitrogenous base** (בסיס חנקני))
	- **adenine** (A)
	- **guanine** (G) 
	- **cytosine** (C)
	- **thymine** (T) 
	- **uracil** (U)
- **Watson–Crick base pair**
- **base pair** (**bp**)
