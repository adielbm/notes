# Lipids

### classical classification

- simple lipids – esters of fatty acids with an alcohol
	- **glycerides** (or **acylglycerols**)
		- **monoglycerides**
		- **diglyceride**
		- **triglyceride** (or **TG**, **triacylglycerol**, **TAG**, or **triacylglyceride**)
			- see [[#fats]]
	- waxes
- complex lipids
	- **phospholipids**
		- consists of
			- hydrophilic head
				- phosphate group
				- alcohol (usually glycerol)
			- two hydrophobic tails 
				- derived from fatty acids
		- amphiphilic
	- **glycolipids**
	- **lipoproteins**
		- types: 
			- **LDL** (low-density lipoprotein)
			- **HDL** (high-density lipoprotein)
			- **Lipoprotein(a)** (or **Lp(a)**)
- derived lipids
	- **[[#fatty acid]]** (חומצת שומן)
	- **glycerol**
	- **[[#steroid]]**

## fats 

- **fat** (שומן)
	- "A lipid consisting of three fatty acids linked to one glycerol molecule; also called a triacylglycerol or triglyceride." [@Campbell, 2020]
	- "Lipid that consists of a glycerol molecule with one, two, or three fatty acid tails. Saturated fats have three saturated fatty acid tails. Unsaturated fats have one or more unsaturated fatty acid tails." [@Starr, 2014]
	- "The terms _saturated_ fats and _unsaturated_ fats are commonly used in the context of nutrition" [@Campbell, 2020]
	- **saturated fat**
	- **unsaturated fat**

# Nucleotides

- **nucleotides**
	- consists of:
		- a nucleotide base
		- [[#Carbohydrate|pentose]] (suger) 
			- e.g. deoxyribose (in DNA), ribose (in RNA) 
		- a phosphate group consisting of one to three phosphates
	- examples: 
		- [[Biochemistry#Coenzymes|ATP]]
		- DNA and RNA nucleotides


- a **nucleotide base** (or **nucleobase**, **nitrogenous base** (בסיס חנקני))
	- **adenine** (A)
	- **guanine** (G) 
	- **cytosine** (C)
	- **thymine** (T) 
	- **uracil** (U)
