
- **reactant** (מגיב)
- **reagent** (מגיב, רֵיאָגנט)

- chemical equation
	- balanced equation
	- molecular equation
	- ionic equation
		- complete ionic equation
		- net ionic equation

- **combination reaction** (תגובת הרכבה) 
	- Two or more reactants combine to form a single product.
	- $\ce{A + B -> AB}$
- **decomposition reaction** (תגובת פירוק)
	- A single reactant breaks down into two or more products.
	- $\ce{AB -> A + B}$	
		- $\ce{ClNa -> Cl + Na}$
		- $\ce{CaCO3(s) ->[\Delta] CaO(s) + CO2(g)}$
- **single displacement reaction** (or **(single) substitution reaction**) (תגובת התמרה יחידה)
	- **double displacement reaction** (or **salt metathesis reaction**) (תגובת התמרה כפולה)

- **combustion reaction**: A substance reacts with oxygen gas, releasing energy in the form of heat and light.
	- $\ce{CH + O2 -> CO2 + H2O}$

- [[Acids and bases|acid–base reaction]]
	- neutralization
- [[redox]]
- **condensation reaction** (תגובת דחיסה)
- **chemical equilibrium** (שיווי משקל כימי, שיווי משקל דינמי)
