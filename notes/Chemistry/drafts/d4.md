### Water in/soluble compounds

| **water soluble compounds contain**                                                                                                    | **exceptions**                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| _alkali metal ions_ $\ce{Li+}$, $\ce{Na+}$, $\ce{K+}$, $\ce{Rb+}$, $\ce{Cs+}$<br>_ammonium ion_ $\ce{NH4+}$                            |                                                                                                                                                |
| _nitrate_ $\ce{NO3-}$ <br>_bicarbonate_ (מימן פחמתי) $\ce{HCO3−}$ <br>_chlorate_ $\ce{ClO3−}$                                          | Chlorate compounds containing $\ce{Cs+}$ and $\ce{Rb+}$ are insoluble.                                                                         |
| _halides_ ($\ce{Cl-}$, $\ce{Br-}$, $\ce{I-}$)                                                                                          | Compounds containing _halides_ of $\ce{Ag+}$ or $\ce{ Hg2^{2+} }$ are insoluble. <br>$\ce{ CuI }$ and $\ce{ PbI2 }$ are also insoluble.        |
| _sulfate_ (גופרה) $\ce{SO4^{2−}}$                                                                                                      | Compounds containing _sulfate_ of $\ce{ Ag+ }$, $\ce{Ca^{2+}}$, $\ce{Sr^{2+}}$, $\ce{Ba^{2+}}$, $\ce{Hg2^{2+}}$, or $\ce{Pb^{2+}}$             |
| **water insoluble compounds contain**                                                                                                  | **exceptions**                                                                                                                                 |
| _carbonate_ (פחמה) $\ce{CO3^{2−}}$ <br>_phosphate_ (זַרְחָה) $\ce{PO4^{3−}}$<br>_chromate_ $\ce{CrO4^{2−}}$<br>_sulfide_ $\ce{S^{2−}}$ | Compounds containing _alkali metal ions_ or the _ammonium ion_ are soluble. <br>$\ce{SrS}$, $\ce{MgCrO4}$, and $\ce{CaCrO4}$ are also soluble. |
| _hydroxide_ $\ce{OH−}$                                                                                                                 | Compounds containing _alkali metal ions_ or the $\ce{Ba^{2+}}$, $\ce{Sr^{2+}}$, or $\ce{Ca^{2+}}$ ions are soluble.                            |
