
- **ionization energy** (IE) (אנרגיית יינון)
	- the minimum energy required to remove an electron from the ground state of the isolated gaseous atom or ion
- _1st ionization energy_ ($I_{1}$): $\ce{X(g) + energy -> X+(g) + e-}$
- _2nd ionization energy_ ($I_{2}$): $\ce{X+(g) + energy -> X^{2+}(g) + e-}$
- _3rd ionization energy_ ($I_{3}$): $\ce{X^{2+}(g) + energy -> X^{3+}(g) + e-}$
- $I_{1}<I_{2}<I_{3}<\dots$
- removing core electrons requires significantly higher IE then removing valence electrons
- periodic trends for $I_1$:
	- higher $I_1$ for higher groups 
	- lower $I_1$ for higher periods
- unit: $\mathrm{kJ/mol}$, $\mathrm{eV}$

# Electron affinity

- **electron affinity** ($E_{\text{ea}}$) (זיקת-אלקטרונים, זיקה אלקטרונית)
	- $\ce{X(g) + e- -> X-(g) + energy}$
	- unit: $\mathrm{kJ/mol}$, $\mathrm{eV}$ 
	- $E_{\text{ea}}>0$, energy released, stable anion formed, when electron attachment is exothermic ($\Delta E<0$)
	- $E_{\text{ea}} \approx 0 \text{ or negative}$, electron attachment is not favorable, electron attachment is endothermic ($\Delta E>0$)
	- $E_{\text{ea}}=E(\ce{X})+E(\ce{e-})-E(\ce{X-})=-\Delta E$
	- Lowest for noble gases and alkali metals
	- Highest for halogens
