- (_discrete_) The **convolution** of two functions $f,g:\mathbb{Z}\to \mathbb{C}$  is a function $f*g:\mathbb{Z}\to \mathbb{C}$ defined as $$(f*g)(n)=\displaystyle \sum _{m\in \mathbb{Z}}f(m)g(n-m).$$
- (_continuous_) The **convolution** of two functions $f,g:\mathbb{R}\to \mathbb{C}$  is a function $f*g:\mathbb{R}\to \mathbb{C}$ defined as $$(f*g)(x)=\displaystyle \int _{-\infty}^{\infty}f(t)g(x-t)dt.$$
