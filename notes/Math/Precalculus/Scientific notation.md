- **scientific notation** (or **scientific form** or **standard index form**, or **standard form**)
	- numbers are written n the form $m\times 10^n$, where:
		- $m\in\mathbb{R}$ (**significand** (or **mantissa** [^1]))
		- $n\in\mathbb{Z}$ (exponent)

[^1]: The term _mantissa_ can also refer to the [[Fractional part]] of the logarithm result.
