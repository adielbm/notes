# Seed

- **seed** (זֶרַע)
- **seed coat** (or **testa**) (קְלִפַּת זֶרַע)
- Gymnosperm:
	- embryo
		- cotyledons: one or more; often several in conifers
	- nutrient-storage tissue: female gametophyte tissue
	- wing (in some species)
- flowring plants:
	- embryo
		- cotyledons: one in monocots, two in eudicots
	- **endosperm** (אֶנְדּוֹסְפֵּרְם)
		- often triploid (3n)
- **cotyledon** (פְּסִיג)
- **plumule** (נצרון, נוצה, נצר עוברי)
- **radicle** (שָׁרְשׁוֹן, שורשון, שורש עוברי)
- **integument** (מַעֲטֵה הַבֵּיצִית)
