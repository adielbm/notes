## Cofactors

- **cofactor** (קוֹפַקְטוֹר)
	- inorganic cofactors: $\ce{Mg^{2+},Zn^{2+},Fe^{2+}}$
	- organic cofactors: **coenzyme** (קוֹאֶנְזִים)

### Coenzymes


- **nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide** 
	- oxidized form:**NAD+**
	- reduced form: **NADH**
- **nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate** 
	- oxidized form: **NADP+**
	- reduced form: **NADPH**
- **flavin adenine dinucleotide**
	- oxidized form: **FAD**
	- reduced form: **FADH2**

- **Acetyl-CoA** (**acetyl coenzyme A**)

- **ATP** (**Adenosine triphosphate**) $\ce{C_{10}H_{16}N_{5}O_{13}P_{3}}$
- **ADP** (**Adenosine diphosphate**) $\ce{C10H15N5O10P2}$
- $\ce{ATP <=> ADP + P_i}$
- $\ce{ATP + H2O -> ADP + P_i}$
- **heme** (or **haem**)
- ascorbic acid
- biotin (vitamin B7)
