# Gametogenesis

- animals:
	- male: **[[#spermatogenesis]]** (יְצִירַת תָּאֵי הַזֶּרַע)
		- 1 primary spermatocyte (2n) → 4 sperm (n)
	- female: **[[#oogenesis]]** (הִתְפַּתְּחוּת הַבֵּיצִית)
		- 1 primary oocyte (2n) → 1 ovum (n) + 2–3 polar bodies, which degenerate
- flowering plants:
	- male: 1 microspore (n) →  Pollen with a tube cell and 2 sperm cells (n)
	- female: 1 megaspore (n) → 3 rounds of mitosis give 8 nuclei in 7 cells, including 1 egg cell (n) and the central cell with 2 polar nuclei

## Spermatogenesis

- **spermatocytogenesis**: 1 spermatogonium (2n) → 1 primary spermatocyte (2n)
	- **spermatogonium** (pl.: **spermatogonia**) (2n) (תָּא אַב-זֶרַע)
	- **spermatocyte** (תָּאֵי זֶרַע) (2n)
	- spermatogonia divide by mitosis: some stay as stem cells (self-renewal), others differentiate into primary spermatocytes
- **spermatidogenesis**: (meiosis): 1 primary spermatocyte (2n) → 4 spermatids (n)
	- meiosis I: 1 primary spermatocyte (2n) → 2 secondary spermatocytes (n, 2 chromatids per chromosome)
	- meiosis II: 2 secondary spermatocytes → 4 spermatids (n, 1 chromatid per chromosome)
		- **spermatid** (קְדַם-זֶרַע) (n)
- **spermiogenesis**: 4 spermatids → 4 spermatozoa (n)
	- no division, only differentiation: acrosome, flagellum, nucleus condenses, most cytoplasm removed
	- **spermatozoon** (pl.: **spermatozoa**) (זֵרְעוֹן) – a sperm cell
	- see also: **spermatia**
- **spermiation**: release of mature spermatozoa from Sertoli cells into the lumen of the seminiferous tubule

## Oogenesis

- **oogonium** (pl.: **oogonia**) (2n)
- **oocytogenesis**: 1 oogonium (2n) → 1 primary oocyte (2n)
	- **oocyte** (בֵּיצִית, תָּא בֵּיצָה) (2n)
- **ootidogenesis (meiosis)**: 1 primary oocyte (2n) → 1 ootid (n) + 2–3 polar bodies
	- meiosis I: 1 primary oocyte (2n) → 1 secondary oocyte (n) + 1 first polar body (n)
	- meiosis II: 1 secondary oocyte (n) → 1 ootid (n) + 1 second polar body (n)
		- **ovulation** (בִּיּוּץ)
		- **polar body** (גּוּפִיף קוֹטְבִי)
		- **ootid** (n)
- 1 ootid → 1 ovum (n)
	- **ovum** (pl.: **ova**) (n)
- **folliculogenesis**
	- **follicle** (זָקִיק)
- result: 1 ovum + 2–3 polar bodies

- **alternation of generations** (or **metagenesis**, **heterogenesis**)
	- **sporophyte** (דּוֹר נִבְגִּי) – "A plant (or the diploid phase in its life cycle) which produces spores by meiosis in order to produce gametophytes." (Wiktionary)
	- **gametophyte** (גָּמֵטוֹפִיט) – "A plant (or the haploid phase in its life cycle) which produces gametes by mitosis in order to produce a zygote." (Wiktionary)
- **sporangium** (מִנְבָּג) (pl. **sporangia**) produces spores which develop into gametophyte 
	- **spore** (נֶבֶג) – a haploid cell (n)
	- female: **megasporangum** (or **macrosporangium**) (מִנְבָּג נְקֵבִי)
		- (in flowering plants: the megasporangum is the [[Plants#Flower|embryo sac]])
		- produces **megaspores** (or **macrospores**) (נֶבֶג נְקֵבִי) 
			- develop into **megagametophyte** (female gametophyte) (גָּמֵטוֹפִיט נְקֵבִי)
	- male: **microsporangium** (מִנְבָּג זְכָרִי)
		- (in flowering plants: the microsporangium is the [[Plants#Flower|pollen grain]] with the pollen tube)
		- produces **microspores** (נֶבֶג זְכָרִי)
			- develop into **microgametophyte** (male gametophyte) (גָּמֵטוֹפִיט זְכָרִי)
