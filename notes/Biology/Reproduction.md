# Life cycles

- **biological life cycle**
	- **haplontic**
		- haploid organism (n) → gametes (n) → zygote (2n) → (initial) meiosis 
	- **diplontic**
		- diploid organism (2n) → (terminal) meiosis → gametes (n) → zygote (2n) 
	- **haplodiplontic**
		- sporophyte (2n) → (intermediate) meiosis → gametophyte (n) → gametes (n) → zygote (2n)
		- isomorphic
		- heteromorphic

## Spermatogenesis

- **spermatocytogenesis**: 1 spermatogonium (2n) → 1 primary spermatocyte (2n)
	- spermatogonia divide by mitosis: some stay as stem cells (self-renewal), others differentiate into primary spermatocytes
- **spermatidogenesis**: (meiosis): 1 primary spermatocyte (2n) → 4 spermatids (n)
	- meiosis I: 1 primary spermatocyte (2n) → 2 secondary spermatocytes (n, 2 chromatids per chromosome)
	- meiosis II: 2 secondary spermatocytes → 4 spermatids (n, 1 chromatid per chromosome)
		- **spermatid** (קְדַם-זֶרַע) (n)
- **spermiogenesis**: 4 spermatids → 4 spermatozoa (n)
	- no division, only differentiation: acrosome, flagellum, nucleus condenses, most cytoplasm removed
	- **spermatozoon** (pl. **spermatozoa**) (זֵרְעוֹן) – a sperm cell
	- see also: **spermatia**
- **spermiation**: release of mature spermatozoa from Sertoli cells into the lumen of the seminiferous tubule

## Oogenesis


- **oocytogenesis**: 1 oogonium (2n) → 1 primary oocyte (2n)
- **ootidogenesis** (meiosis): 1 primary oocyte (2n) → 1 ootid (n) + 2–3 polar bodies
	- meiosis I: 1 primary oocyte (2n) → 1 secondary oocyte (n) + 1 first polar body (n)
	- meiosis II: 1 secondary oocyte (n) → 1 ootid (n) + 1 second polar body (n)
		- [[#Menstrual cycle|ovulation]]
		- **polar body** (גּוּפִיף קוֹטְבִי)
		- **ootid** (n)
- 1 ootid → 1 ovum (n)
	- **ovum** (pl. **ova**) (n)
- result: 1 ovum + 2–3 polar bodies
