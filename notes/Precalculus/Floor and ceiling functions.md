- The **floor** (also called the **integral part**, **integer part**, **greatest integer**, or **entier**) of a real number $x$ is the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$. 
	- (**floor function**) $\text{floor}(x)=\displaystyle  \lfloor x\rfloor :=\max\{m\in \mathbb {Z} \mid m\leq x\}$
	- $x-1<\lfloor x\rfloor\leq{x}< \lfloor x\rfloor+1$
	- $n<x<n+1\implies n=\lfloor x \rfloor$ for $n\in\mathbb{N}$
- The **ceiling** of a real number $x$ is the least integer greater than or equal to $x$.
	- (**ceiling function**) $\text{ceil}(x)=\displaystyle  \lceil x\rceil :=\min\{n\in \mathbb {Z} \mid n\geq x\}$
